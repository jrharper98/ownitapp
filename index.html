What is OwnIt?

All your business expenses,
one simplified app.

No App Store. No subscriptions. Just your data — organized, branded, and ready for tax season.

OwnIt is a mobile-first web app that lives on your phone's Home Screen. Open it like any app — no download required.

Track expenses, income and events across all your businesses from one beautifully designed dashboard, customized just for you. At tax time, export a Schedule C summary or CSV straight to your accountant — no scrambling, no guesswork.

Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

Built around how real entrepreneurs actually work —
not how accountants wish they did.

Multiple business tabs

A separate tab for each income stream. All your businesses in one app — cleanly organized.

Expense logging

Date, vendor, category, amount, payment method and receipt photos — logged in seconds.

Income YTD + net profit

Log platform income and watch your net profit calculate in real time, per business.

Tax form record keeping

Store your 1099-NEC and 1099-K forms right inside your app. Tax season, sorted.

CSV + Schedule C export

One-tap export to a spreadsheet or an IRS-ready Schedule C summary for your accountant.

Multi-device cloud sync

Optional Supabase cloud sync keeps your data seamless across phone, tablet, and laptop.
